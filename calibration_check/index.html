LEADER–FOLLOWER TEST

Calibration test readiness

Waiting for joint readings…

The follower immediately follows the leader, without startup alignment or an application speed cap. Large pose differences can cause sudden motion. Clear the path and keep the elbow bent.

Pose gaps, fast movement, tracking lag, and proximity to joint limits produce warnings. Stop & hold ends the test and retains holding torque. Motor faults and lost communication still stop commands.

The current calibration is kept as a checkpoint before changes. New calibrations are saved here too, so you can switch back later.

Use a saved checkpoint to restore both arms and its matching reset pose, or start the guided center-and-range calibration. Either action releases torque. Keep both arms supported throughout.

FOLLOWER / MOTOR 6 ONLY

Gripper force

Adjust the gripper torque limit while the other joints keep following. Percentage sets the motor output limit; it is not a force measurement in newtons.

Reading motor 6…

Support the object when changing force: a lower setting can let it slip. A lower setting can still overload if the target keeps squeezing against the object. An existing overload still needs Clear Error & Reset.

This setting lasts until motor power is cycled. Open this panel again to read it after reconnecting. Calibration and motor protection settings are preserved.

RELEASE MOTOR TORQUE
